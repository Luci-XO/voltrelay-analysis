VoltRelay Energy · Why service, retention and margin slipped while volume grew

Jan 2024 – Jun 2025 · 3.81M swap attempts · 150 stations · 6 cities · Data Analytics Hackathon (Gradient Learnings)

Story in one line: failures spike every summer at old Gen1 chargers in hot cities. Those failures drive new-rider churn, while a bad battery batch and a bigger fleet discount squeeze margin.
